Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first class at AV Dance Studio.

Where is AV Dance Studio located?

884 High Road, North Finchley, London N12 9RH.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 10:00-21:00.

How much is an introductory lesson?

£20 for a single New Student Introductory lesson, or £25 for a couple.

Do I need a dance partner or previous experience?

No. Our beginner lessons welcome total beginners, with or without a partner.

Do you offer lessons for a wedding first dance?

Yes. Our Wedding Package includes 3 personal lessons for £180.

When are your group classes?

Every Wednesday and Saturday, £15 per person — see Group Classes for more.

Do you offer gift certificates?

Yes, from £35 up to £260 (£280 for couples) — see our full Gift Certificates pricing.

Do you host parties or social events?

Yes. Children's parties, adult Latino nights, and studio hire — see Extras for details.

Can I rent the studio space?

Yes. Contact us for details and availability on renting our main studio.

How do I book or get in touch?

Call +44 7717 380266, email info@avdancestudio.co.uk, or fill out the contact form.
