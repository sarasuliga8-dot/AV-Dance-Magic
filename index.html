About Our Studio

Welcome to AV Dance Studio - North Finchley's home for ballroom, Latin, and social dance classes in the heart of North London!

Whether you're a total beginner or a seasoned dancer, we invite you to step onto the dance floor and join our vibrant dance community. From elegant ballroom to fiery Latin moves, our classes are packed with fun, energy, and plenty of support to help you shine.

Dancing with us isn't just about learning steps-it's about gaining confidence, making friends, and boosting your health along the way. Our North London studio welcomes dancers of every level, from complete beginners to couples preparing for their wedding first dance.

Let's groove, twirl, and laugh together as you discover the joy of dance.

So, what are you waiting for? Come dance with us in North Finchley and feel the rhythm of life!

Ready to dance? Join us now.
